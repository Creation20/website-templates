Our story

My grandmother’s kitchen, with better lighting.

Pepper & Palm started as a Sunday lunch in Labone, cooked over firewood in clay pots the way Efua’s grandmother did it in Cape Coast. Five years on, nothing about the cooking has changed. We just added chairs.

“If the light soup doesn’t make you sweat a little, I haven’t done my job.”— Chef Efua Mensah
